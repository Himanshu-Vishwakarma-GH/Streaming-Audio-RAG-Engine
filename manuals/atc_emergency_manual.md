# Air Traffic Control Emergency Manual & Numeric Tolerances

## Section 1: Altitude & Separation
- Minimum radar separation: 3 nautical miles within terminal airspace, 5 nautical miles en-route.
- Emergency descent target altitude: 10,000 feet MSL or lowest safe altitude (MEA).
- Transponder Squawk codes:
  - Hijacking: 7500
  - Radio Failure (NORDO): 7600
  - General Emergency: 7700

## Section 2: Chemical Plant Emergency Overpressure
- Reactor Core R-101 Maximum Allowable Working Pressure (MAWP): 450.0 PSI.
- Emergency venting trip threshold: 485.5 PSI.
- Quench tank injection rate: 1250 liters/min of water-glycol solution.
